3. What each word means

Context — why you need this, and who will read it.
Role — who the AI should act like.
Task — what you want the AI to make.
Format — how it should look when it's done.

4. 5 ways to fix an answer

The first answer is not always right. Use one of these 5 tricks to fix it.

Answer
Pick a trick
Better answer

Fix tricks 1–3

Tap to learn each one.

Answer covers too much? Ask for just one part.

“Only tell me about ___.”

Answer too short? Ask for more detail.

“Tell me more about ___.”

Words too hard, or too simple? Ask AI to change how it sounds.

“Say this in a simple way for ___.”

Fix tricks 4–5

Answer sounds sure, but might be wrong? Ask AI to check itself.

“What might be wrong here?”

Got 2 good answers? Ask AI to combine the best parts.

“Combine the best parts of both.”
When to stop

If fixing it yourself is faster than asking again — just fix it.
